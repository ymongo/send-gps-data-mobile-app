Initial release of Send GPS Data: privacy-first, self-hosted GPS location streaming for Android.

- Stream your live location over WebSocket to any server you own (ws:// or wss://)
- QR scan of a server URL with the camera (ZXing, open source)
- Save & switch between favorite servers
- Built-in Bitcoin (Lightning) tip widget
- F-Droid friendly: standard Android Location API, no proprietary dependencies
